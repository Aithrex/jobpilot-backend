Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. This policy explains what My Private Mail does with your email and data.

What the service does

My Private Mail provides a permanent forwarding email address. Mail sent to your address is scanned for malware and phishing, then forwarded to the personal inbox you verified.

What we store

We store the metadata needed to deliver and organize your mail (sender, subject, timestamps) and the message contents you choose to keep. We do not sell your data or use message content for advertising.

Security

Messages and attachments are encrypted at rest. Attachments are validated by file signature and quarantined if suspicious. HTML is sanitized before it is shown to you.

Your control

You can pause, rotate, or disable your address, change your forwarding destination (with re-verification), and request deletion of your data at any time.
