Why a forwarding address?

Every time you sign up somewhere or fill out a form, your personal email gets copied, shared, and sometimes leaked. A forwarding address is a permanent alias you control: messages still reach you, but your real inbox stays private.

Privacy first

We only forward legitimate mail to your verified personal inbox. We don't sell data, we don't spam, and messages are scanned for malware and phishing before they reach you. You can disable an address at any time.
