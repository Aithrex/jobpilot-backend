## Scope and limits - read first

- **What this is:** 137 Jobright recommendation cards across 100 displayed company names, captured 2026-10-21 (America/Phoenix) by scrolling ONE user's currently filtered recommendations top to bottom. Filters in effect: `United States`, `DevOps (+14)`, `Entry Level (+1)`, `Full-time (+1)`, `Onsite (+2)`, `Past 24 hours`, `Artificial Intelligence (AI) (+2)`, `0-2 Years`.
- **What it is not:** all of Jobright, a random sample, or JobPilot inventory. Nothing here is a JobPilot measurement, and no JobPilot metric may be computed from it.
- **No fraud is established.** Reviewer `fraud_verdict` values: `not_determined` x137. Official requisitions verified: 0/137. Legitimate service firms are not scams; a consultancy label is a PREFERENCE category.
- **No revenue, hiring-intent or ranking-quality claims** about Jobright. Ghost hiring intent is unobservable from a card; an original link is an observed outbound link, not proof of ownership or that the role is open.
- Counts below are only what the card fields, reviewer labels and links support. Every figure carries its denominator.
